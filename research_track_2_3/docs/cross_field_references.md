# Cross-field references imported from project chat

These are citation records and proposed applications copied from that chat, not downloaded paper PDFs or a new full-text verification. Applications below are our adaptations, not validated AI-collusion measures from these papers.

| Reference | Connection to the proposed framework |
|---|---|
| Porter & Zona (1999), [Ohio School Milk Markets: An Analysis of Bidding](https://www.nber.org/papers/w6037) | Separate evidence of agreement from economic damages against a competitive comparison. |
| Hernán & Robins (2016), [Using Big Data to Emulate a Target Trial When a Randomized Trial Is Not Available](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4832051/) | Specify intervention, comparator, assignment and outcome before claiming causal harm. |
| Hudgens & Halloran (2008), [Toward Causal Inference With Interference](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2600548/) | Separate effects on coalition members, outsider spillovers and overall outcomes. Static outsider-fixed comparisons do not estimate all these effects. |
| Obermeyer et al. (2019), [Dissecting Racial Bias in an Algorithm Used to Manage the Health of Populations](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31649194/) | Audit the validity of reward as a proxy for the harm endpoint; reward inequality and task loss are different constructs. |
| CAST Investigators (1989), [Effect of Encainide and Flecainide on Mortality in a Randomized Trial of Arrhythmia Suppression](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJM198908103210629) | Distinguish intermediate indicators from the outcome that matters; plan execution or coalition reward is not a validated surrogate for harm. |
| Manski (1990), [Nonparametric Bounds on Treatment Effects](https://ipcid.org/evaluation/outros_temas/Manski%20-%20Nonparametric%20Bounds.pdf) | Motivate explicit uncertainty under incomplete identification. Our optimal-allocation ranges are benchmark-sensitivity ranges, not Manski causal bounds. |
