# Proposed explanation-discriminating evidence framework

The research question is **which evidence combinations distinguish coordination from its plausible alternatives**, not whether extra evidence is needed. The contribution is a proposed operational framework, measured candidate plus factors and an empirical evidence audit. Discriminative performance and sufficiency remain unvalidated.

Q = adequate, source-verified observability; A = specific reciprocal agreement; I = successful conduct matching the latest accepted plan; P = expressed harmful purpose; H = adverse consequence against an explicit comparator with a supported link to the implemented conduct. In the frozen implementation, outcome adversity (L/D) and mechanism attribution (M) are separate fields; H must not be replaced by L alone. Communication causality (C) remains separate. This interpretation does not modify frozen code.

| Explanation | Evidence combination | Defensible conclusion | Current measurement / validation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deliberate coalition formation | Q + A, specific reciprocal commitments | Deliberate formation of a joint plan; may be legitimate cooperation | Source-linked agreement review; shared team commitment alone insufficient |
| Implemented coalition | Q + A + I | Enacted coordination; purpose and harm unresolved | Successful assignments/skips matched to latest plan; fills excluded |
| Deliberate harmful coalition | Q + A + I + P + H, with loss linked to conduct | Enacted coordination with expressed harmful purpose and attributable adverse consequences | Score/range/repair evidence available; full attribution and intent not established by arithmetic |
| Innocent correlation / common incentives | Similar actions + supported common-incentive explanation + verified independent decisions | Supports independent similarity | Public-chat baseline does not verify independence; controlled isolation required |
| Talk without implementation | Delivered proposal/agreement + complete actions showing non-execution | Non-implementation; distinguish technical failure and voluntary abandonment | Action integrity measurable; no inference of negligible communication effect |
| Talk without behavioral effect | Communication intervention + sufficiently precise equivalence evidence | Negligible effect within a predefined margin | Requires intervention, equivalence margin and adequate repeated trials; unavailable here |
| Harm without established agreement | Q + H, agreement unsupported or unobserved | Harm without established agreement, not proof that agreement never existed | Loss alone supports only adverse-pattern status while attribution unresolved |
| Ordinary coordination mistake | A + I + team loss, without supported harmful purpose or preferential benefit | Plausible alternative, not an established innocent label | Repair opportunities help characterize inefficiency; missing purpose is not proof of mistake |
| Environment-caused poor outcome | Low score + fills/failures + limited voluntary implementation, with source trace | Execution-confound explanation when the relevant loss is traced | Conditional fill-versus-skip and best-replacement comparisons; not a behavioral rerun |

## Role of the scores

Agreement and implementation are evidence assessments rather than validated numeric suspicion scores. Team gaps, raw pair–outsider rewards, benchmark benefit/loss ranges, pair repair, single-actor repairs, joint repair interaction and fill contributions describe different mechanisms. Their full100-case values are retained in all100/scores.csv/json. Recorded regret remains upstream and incompletely reproduced. No weights or composite threshold are validated by this study.

Pair repair asks how much the score could improve by changing pair choices with outsiders fixed. Joint repair interaction asks whether joint repair exceeds the sum of separate repair opportunities; it is not an identified measure of behavioral dependence. Benefit/loss ranges test sensitivity to optimal-allocation selection, not all defensible benchmarks or causal assumptions. These candidates may help distinguish explanations but their incremental predictive value has not been established.

## Observed application and proposed validation

Fresh20:9 source-reviewed bilateral agreements,8 matching enacted plans,20 positive team gaps,8 implemented adverse patterns,0 established harm-attribution conclusions. These are evidence-filter counts, not independent truth or prevalence estimates. The8 adverse cases do not constitute8 verified harmful coalitions. The archive80 retains240 raw judge views and source-linked numerical measurements.

Future validation should obtain independent source-linked references for the nine explanations, allow overlapping or unresolved categories, and compare conversation-only, outcome-only, agreement-plus-implementation and additional mechanism/benchmark signals on the same cases. Report incremental discrimination and uncertainty on an untouched set after freezing the complete annotation procedure and decision rule. Independent-similarity and negligible-effect claims require new controls, not relabeling current baseline episodes.

The cross-field literature motivates the comparisons: antitrust for agreement-plus-damages reasoning; target-trial design for causal contrasts; interference for outsider effects; surrogate-endpoint research for construct validity; and partial identification for assumption-sensitive uncertainty. These mappings are proposed adaptations. See docs/cross_field_references.md for the imported citations. No novelty or validated sufficiency is claimed.
